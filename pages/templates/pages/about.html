About us

Built by one student who wanted parking to feel effortless.

{{ site.SITE_NAME }} started as a BITM university project focused on a very ordinary problem: finding a parking spot in Dhangadhi and across Nepal shouldn't take longer than the errand you came for. This page covers why it was built, what the system actually does, and who is behind it.

Our mission

Turning parking lots into visible, reservable infrastructure

Many lots in Nepal still track occupancy with a notebook, if at all. Drivers can't tell whether a lot is full until they're at the gate, and lot owners have no easy way to see demand patterns or collect digital payments. Our goal with this project was to fix that end to end: a live slot map, a reservation system with transparent pricing, and payment options people in Nepal already trust — eSewa, Khalti, and cash.
